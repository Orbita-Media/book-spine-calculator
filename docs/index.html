How is the spine width of a book calculated?

Spine width = number of pages × thickness of one page, plus the cover allowance some printers add. The page count is always even, because every sheet has two sides; an odd count is rounded up. The paper decides the thickness per page, so the same book on cream paper has a wider spine than on white.

How big is the cover file?

A paperback cover is one file: back cover, spine and front cover side by side, with bleed all round. Width = bleed + trim width + spine + trim width + bleed; height = bleed + trim height + bleed. International print uses 0.125 in (3.175 mm) of bleed, European print 5 mm. Keep text and logos at least that far inside the trim edge and the spine folds.

When can I print text on the spine?

Only when the spine is wide enough. In international print, books under 48 pages carry no spine text. On wider spines, keep 1/16 in (1.59 mm) clear on the left and right of the text, on narrow spines 1/32 in.

Where does the ISBN barcode go?

On the back cover, usually at the bottom near the spine. The calculator reserves a white field of 44.45 × 32.93 mm, 15 mm from the spine fold and 10 mm from the bottom edge, large enough for the bars, the digits and the ISBN line above. The barcode itself is the ISBN-13 encoded as EAN-13; an ISBN-10 is converted to its 978 form automatically.

Questions

Does the spine width differ between printers?

Slightly, because paper stocks differ. Use the figures here to plan, and build the final cover on the template of the printer that prints your book.

Do I need the price add-on?

Many US retailers expect it; elsewhere it is optional. 5 + price in cents means US dollars (52495 = $24.95), 90000 means no price is given.

Is the barcode print-ready?

It is drawn as vectors at nominal EAN size (0.33 mm per module), black on white. Place it on a white field at 100 % size and do not stretch it.

Who checks my cover before printing?

If you publish with ShelfPublisher, the portal checks page size, bleed, spine width and the ISBN in the barcode on upload and tells you what to fix before the release check.
